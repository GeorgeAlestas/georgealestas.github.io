05 — Apps

Apps

Two small macOS tools I built for my own research workflow. Both are free to download.

arXiv Digest

v0.1.0 · menu bar app

Each day’s new arXiv listings in your categories, ranked against your own papers, right in the menu bar.

  • Daily listings for any arXiv categories, refreshed after each announcement
  • Ranks papers on your Mac against your own papers, keywords and followed authors; nothing leaves your Mac except requests to arXiv
  • Tick papers and press ⌘E to export BibTeX to the clipboard and your .bib file
  • Readable LaTeX in titles and abstracts (ΛCDM, H0), light and dark mode

macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon & Intel · 0.8 MB

Download for macOS
First launch

Unzip and move ArxivDigest.app to Applications. The app isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so the first time macOS will refuse to open it: right-click the app → Open → Open.

SciPeek

v1.0.1 · Quick Look extension

Press Space in Finder to preview FITS, HDF5, NumPy, Jupyter and Mathematica files.

  • FITS images with stretch and colormaps, header cards and tables
  • HDF5 trees, NumPy arrays, and rendered Jupyter and Mathematica notebooks
  • Statistics and a histogram for every numeric view, plus data thumbnails in Finder
  • Pure Swift and sandboxed, with no network access

macOS 14+ · Apple Silicon & Intel · 7 MB

Download for macOS
First launch

Unzip, move SciPeek.app to Applications and open it. It isn’t notarized by Apple yet, so macOS will say it can’t verify the developer: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway. Then select a supported file in Finder and press Space.
